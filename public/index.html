2. Watch for blueprints

In Chrome or Edge, select Start capture, then choose Window → ARC Raiders in the browser’s share picker. If the game is absent, use borderless windowed mode or choose Entire Screen.

Blueprint sightings and map locations appear in the tracker. Windows notifications are optional.

Press E to open a container. The scanner spots blueprints there; hover for the name. Then press M and keep the in-game map open until the map name, floor, and location are captured. Uncertain floors stay unpinned for review. Frames stay local.
